## Acceptance criteria
1. 5Cs Depth: Customers by jobs and problems (not demographics); Category trends present; Competition with positioning; Culture as one CVF type tied to execution; one specific unfair advantage tested with all four VRIO tests, honestly.
2. Risks and Resources: non-obvious strategic risks (adoption, culture fit, competitor response, dependencies), each with a realistic mitigation; not a best case; resources sized.
3. Success Metrics: outcomes, not outputs; each with a number, a baseline and a timeframe.
4. Strategic Coherence: every plan element follows from a named finding in the Context; the plan solves the customer pains identified.
5. Future State: a clear, concise, aspirational vision with customer and business impact; success for the company and for the customer.
6. Internal consistency: the canvas (section 1, page 2), the product description (section 2), the analysis sections, the figures and the conclusion say the same thing. Same vision, same unfair advantage, same gate rules, same metric definitions and numbers everywhere.
7. Evidence: every figure has a source in the reference list or is marked (A) and listed under Assumptions. No invented facts.
8. Format: order Current Context, Future State, Plan; all ten canvas prompts answered; PDF or Word; one product; the completed canvas comes first.
9. Product clarity: a reader who does not know telecom can tell what kind of product Yaqoot is (software app, packages, connection), what the proposal develops in each part (section 2.1), and in which order it is built (roadmap in section 5.1).
