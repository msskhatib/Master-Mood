    Objective
    This is an individual assignment that will be evaluated by the Learning Facilitator.

    The objective is to develop a comprehensive Product Strategy Canvas that demonstrates your ability to analyze a
    product’s current context, define a compelling future vision, and outline a coherent strategic plan.

    You are expected to apply key frameworks such as the 5Cs, VRIO, and Competing Values Framework (CVF) to
    produce a strategy that is insightful, realistic, and outcome-driven. To do so, you will complete the Product Strategy
    Canvas (to download your editable version, click below):

    A2.1 Strategy Canvas Template-2.pptx (https://impactonline.ie.edu/courses/1766/files/206374?wrap=1)
    (https://impactonline.ie.edu/courses/1766/files/206374/download?download_frd=1)


    Instructions
    You will structure your work across 3 main sections:

    Section 1 - Current Context. Provide a clear and insightful analysis of:

        Customers: Define target users using a needs-based approach (focus on problems and jobs-to-be-done, not
        demographics).
        Competitors: Identify key competitors and their positioning.
        Capabilities: Assess your organization’s strengths using the VRIO framework (Value, Rarity, Imitability,
        Organization).
        Culture: Identify your organizational culture using the Competing Values Framework (Clan, Adhocracy, Market, or
        Hierarchy).

    Section 2 - Future State

    First, define a Product Vision that is:

        Clear and concise
        Aspirational
        Focused on the impact on the customer and the business

    Next, describe what success looks like:

        For the company
        For the customer

    Section 3 - The Plan

        Capabilities & Resources: Identify the key resources required to execute the strategy.
        Risks & Mitigation: Highlight potential risks and propose realistic mitigation strategies.
        Success Metrics: Define measurable outcomes (not outputs) that will indicate success.
