# Teacher's posts (Christina Mack)

## Final checks before submitting

1. 5Cs: go deep (30 of 75 points, your biggest section)
   - Customers: describe them by the jobs they need done and the problems they face, not by demographics.
   - Include Category trends. The rubric scores them even though the brief doesn't list them separately.
   - Use VRIO to find one real unfair advantage. Test it honestly: is it actually rare and hard to copy?
   - Name your culture type (CVF) and explain what it means for how you'll carry out the strategy.
2. Risks: think beyond budget and time (20 points)
   - Look for the less obvious strategic risks, like adoption, culture fit, competitor response or dependencies.
   - Give each risk a realistic mitigation. A plan that only covers the best case won't score well.
3. Metrics: measure outcomes, not outputs (15 points)
   - "Launch the app" is an output. "Reduce churn by 10%" is an outcome.
   - Make every metric measurable: a number, a baseline and a timeframe.
4. Coherence: connect everything (10 points)
   - Your Plan should follow directly from your Context. Ask yourself: does my plan solve the customer pain I identified?
5. Don't skip the Future State. The rubric doesn't score it directly, but the brief asks for it. Write a clear, ambitious vision, and describe what success looks like for both the customer and the company.

Final check: every section of the canvas is complete, it follows Context, Future State, Plan, and it's submitted as a PDF or Word file.

## On choosing a product (16 November)

1. Building on earlier coursework is fine. You can use a concept from a previous course. Just say where it came from, and make sure the work you submit here is new: the 5Cs, VRIO, culture, vision, plan and metrics.
2. Your product doesn't have to be from your employer. You can analyse a company you don't work for, using public sources and your own market knowledge. Please note your sources and any assumptions you make.
