# A2.1 Tech Product Management Rubric (75 points)

| Criterion | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Inadequate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current Context: 5Cs Depth (30) | 30 pts. Deep, insightful analysis of Customers (JTBD), Category trends, Competition, and Culture. Identifies a specific "Unfair Advantage" (VRIO). | 15 pts. Clear analysis of all 5Cs. Identifies standard strengths and market players. | 8 pts. Covers most 5Cs but remains surface-level. Analysis feels generic. | 0 pts. Fails to address 5Cs. No clear understanding of the market or organizational context. |
| The Plan: Risks & Resources (20) | 20 pts. Identifies non-obvious strategic risks and provides pragmatic mitigation plans. Resource needs are realistic. | 15 pts. Identifies standard risks (e.g., budget, time) with basic mitigation strategies. | 8 pts. Risks are mentioned but mitigation is weak or unrealistic. | 0 pts. Ignores risks or provides a purely optimistic "best-case" scenario. |
| The Plan: Success Metrics (15) | 15 pts. Focuses on Outcomes (e.g., "Reduce churn by 10%") rather than Outputs (e.g., "Launch app"). Metrics are measurable. | 10 pts. Success is defined by a mix of outcomes and project milestones. | 5 pts. Success is defined only by completing the project or hitting dates. | 0 pts. No clear way to measure if the strategy is succeeding. |
| Strategic Coherence (10) | 10 pts. The "Plan" is a direct logical consequence of the "Context." Every element is aligned. | 5 pts. The plan generally aligns with the context, with minor gaps in logic. | 3 pts. The plan and context feel disconnected (e.g., the plan doesn't solve the identified user pain). | 0 pts. The document feels like a collection of random facts with no unifying logic. |

Target: the Excellent level of every criterion.
