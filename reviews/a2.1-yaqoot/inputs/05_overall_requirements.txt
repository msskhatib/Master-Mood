8. The linked article (Figure 1 in the brief)
The brief shows one figure from the Product School article "3 Essential Product Strategy Frameworks for Product Success" (Form, Educate, Embed). It adds no requirement. Its wording supports
three phrases of the brief: the vision as a "guiding star" that outlines "the desired impact on customers and the market"; a strategy that is "coherent"; and "a robust system for measuring
outcomes". The article does not explain the 5Cs, VRIO or CVF.

9. Honest notes on the course match
1. The 5Cs, jobs to be done, category, capabilities, competition, culture and vision are taught in the workbook (sections 1.4.1, 2.2.1 to 2.2.7, 2.3.1 to 2.3.9).
2. VRIO and the four CVF types are named in the brief only. No captured lesson explains them.
3. Lessons 3.3.3 (Outcomes, Not Features) and 3.4.1 (Strategy Is Saying No) are empty placeholders in the workbook. The same ideas appear in 2.2.1.
4. There is no lesson on risk. Risks are derived from the 5Cs and the teacher's comment.
5. Live sessions are not captured.
