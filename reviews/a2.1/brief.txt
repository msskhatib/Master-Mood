    Assignment Instructions
    Please note: This is an individual assignment.

    Learning Objectives

    By completing this assessment, you will:

        Apply core leadership concepts in a scenario-based context
        Practice cultural interpretation and inclusive decision-making
        Demonstrate awareness of communication signals and team dynamics
        Reflect on how your leadership approach can adapt in multicultural environments

    Scenario: Resetting the Team

    Your team is part of an international digital transformation unit at a global tech company. You are three weeks into a
    project sprint to launch a new internal tool, but team dynamics are starting to break down.

    Context:

        One team member, based in India, has stopped contributing during meetings and has missed two soft deadlines.
        A team member in Germany continues to escalate issues to management, insisting that decision-making is too
        slow.
        The US-based product lead is becoming frustrated and has proposed removing one or more team members.
        You, as the designated team leader, must reorient the project's leadership approach before it derails completely.

    Your job is to step in, clarify the leadership strategy, and get the team back on track.

    Assessment Structure
    You will work through the three decision points below, analyzing them from multiple leadership perspectives in a
    comprehensive analysis.

     Decision Points:

        Communication Reset: How will you improve clarity and ensure psychological safety across cultures and time
        zones?
        Delegation and Empowerment: How do you reassign responsibilities while keeping the team motivated and
        culturally respected?
        Conflict Response: How do you address the tension between speed and inclusion in decision-making?

    Submission Requirements
    Part 1: Leadership Decision Analysis (500 words maximum)

    For each of the three decision points, provide:

        Your decision and rationale (150-200 words per decision point)

    Part 2: Multi-Perspective Analysis (400 words maximum)

    Analyze the scenario from these four critical perspectives:

    Cultural Interpreter Perspective (100 words):

        Identify how cultural dynamics influence each team member's behavior
        Reference specific cultural frameworks (power distance, communication styles, decision-making preferences)

    Signal Reader Perspective (100 words):

        Identify unspoken needs, tensions, and behavioral patterns
        Analyze what each team member's actions communicate beyond their words

    Strategic Evaluator Perspective (100 words):

        Assess how your decisions support long-term project goals
        Evaluate potential risks and benefits of your leadership approach

    Change Management Perspective (100 words):

        Analyze how to implement your decisions while maintaining team cohesion
        Address resistance and cultural adaptation challenges

    Part 3: Leadership Reflection (200 words)

    Reflect on:

        What you learned about leading across cultures through this analysis
