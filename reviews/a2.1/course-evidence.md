# Course evidence (HPL Workbook v22), checked by the host

Short excerpts the host verified in the course workbook text. Line numbers refer to the host's text extraction and are for traceability only.

- Servant leadership (2.2.3; team dynamics): "Key characteristics of servant leadership include prioritizing team members' growth, active listening and empathy..." (l.4917). "When a servant leader guides a team, we typically observe: 1. Enhanced psychological safety..." (l.5297-5299).
- Empowering leadership: "delegating authority, not just tasks, encouraging autonomous decision-making..." (l.4943).
- Transactional leadership: "clear expectations, clear rewards approach... leading a team with a tight deadline. Setting clear expectations and having regular check-ins can make all the difference." (l.5075-5082).
- Inclusive decision-making: "actively seek input from all team members, explain the reasoning behind decisions, create transparent processes for decision-making, and balance team involvement with decisive action." (l.5330-5333).
- GLOBE: "nine dimensions, including... Humane Orientation... performance orientation" (l.4222-4225); "power distance", "in-group collectivism" (l.4699-4700). No country scores are given.
- Hofstede: power distance, "how comfortable folks are with hierarchy... In some cultures, challenging your boss's ideas is normal. In others, it would be unthinkable." (l.4210-4212).
- India's NT model: "Indian Approach: The Nurturant-Task Leader. India's NT model presents leadership as a family relationship... combines care and affection with clear expectations." (l.4715-4719). The course does not name Sinha.
- BLUF: "bottom line up front, or BLUF... Give people the most important information first" (l.4087-4089). Recap: "end every meeting with a clear recap. Have 1 team member summarize the key points orally and rotate this responsibility" (l.4096-4098). Stated steps (l.4099).
- Communication bridge: "Before you can share information or provide feedback, you need to establish a connection... 'I noticed you've been putting in extra effort on this project.'" (l.5973-5979).
- PAUSE: "Pause before responding... Acknowledge your emotions... Understand others' perspectives... Select your response thoughtfully... Engage with empathy." (l.2131-2136).
- Emotional intelligence: "four pillars of EI" (l.2379); Goleman's model with four components (l.2481-2498); "relationship management being the final component of the emotional intelligence model" (l.3084).
- Active listening: course topic 1.3.1 "Active listening tools" (l.1808).
- Leader humility: 1.3.11 (l.4284-4300).
