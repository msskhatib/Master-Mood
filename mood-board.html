Mood Board 02

Mood boards bring together visual elements, colours and styles to define the look and feel of a brand and guide its visual identity.

DIRECTION

Sahra & Sage is a boutique desert retreat in AlUla, Saudi Arabia. Luxury tents, open skies and warm Arabian hospitality. A place to disconnect, breathe, stargaze and reconnect with what matters.

TYPOGRAPHY

An elegant, high-contrast serif paired with a clean geometric sans-serif and an Arabic calligraphic accent. It feels timeless yet modern, like heritage meeting contemporary luxury.

COLOURS

An earthy palette taken from the desert itself. Sand and dune tones make a warm base, terracotta and date brown add depth, and soft sage and night indigo bring calm and a hint of magic.

IMAGERY

Golden dunes, sandstone canyons, starlit tents, cosy interiors and slow coffee moments. The focus is on light, texture, silence and the feeling of space.

EMOJI

The palm tree emoji 🌴 stands for what matters most to me: home, generosity and the date palm that has welcomed guests across Arabia for centuries.

BRAND WORDS

Serene, warm, grounded, authentic, luxurious, soulful, timeless, restorative.

NOTES

Sahra & Sage should feel like an oasis. Guests arrive tired and leave restored. Earthy, calm and quietly luxurious: a desert escape for slow mornings and starry nights.
